# Proposal: Connected-Component Logo Glyph Extraction & Dark-Plate App Share Icon Suite

## Summary
Standardizes connected-component logo glyph extraction (isolating a sub-glyph such as the Brick Labor claw hammer from adjacent wordmark letters via 8-connected alpha flood-fill + 8x Mitchell-Netravali SDF contour vectorization) against a canonical dark brand plate (`#0A0E14` / `#060610`) across `/favicon.svg`, `/favicon.ico`, `/apple-touch-icon.png`, `/android-chrome-192x192.png`, `/android-chrome-512x512.png`, `/mstile-150x150.png`, `/icon-16.png`..`/icon-512.png`, `/icon-maskable-512.png`, and social OG share cards.
